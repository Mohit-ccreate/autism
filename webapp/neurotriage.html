Methodological Tautology Warning: Label Leakage in Behavioral Datasets

In published machine learning papers on the UCI and Kaggle autism questionnaire datasets, the target label was simply an arithmetic step function of the questionnaire item scores ($\sum_{i=1}^{10} A_i \ge 7 \iff \text{Screen-Positive}$). Complex neural networks and gradient boosting models achieve trivial 99%–100% accuracy because they are simply reconstructing this addition rule.

Confound Finding: Connectome features decode scanner identity with 64.87% top-1 accuracy (vs 19.91% majority-site base rate, 5.0% chance), explaining why random CV leaks scanner identity across train/test splits.
